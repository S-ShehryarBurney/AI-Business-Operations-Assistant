COMPANY RETURN POLICY

Customers can return unused products within 14 days of delivery.

Products must be in their original condition and packaging.

Damaged products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Customers should contact customer support before sending a product back.


COMPANY REFUND POLICY

Approved refunds are processed after the returned product has been inspected.

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing can take 5 to 7 business days after approval.


COMPANY SHIPPING POLICY

Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 5 business days.

Customers receive tracking information after their order has been shipped.

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, the customer should contact support.


COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Customer support handles return, refund, shipping, and order-related questions.

Customers should provide their order ID when contacting support about a specific order.
